# Feature brainstorm — a gentle game for real life

## Included now
1. **Daily rituals, not guilt:** flexible habit days, numeric progress, scheduled-day streaks, and no XP loss for missed days.
2. **Tasks as quests:** effort-based XP, checklists, recurring occurrences, and board/list modes.
3. **A personal progression loop:** habits/tasks → XP → levels → virtual coins → self-chosen rewards.
4. **Daily side quests:** three simple bonuses that encourage a balanced day instead of endless checking.
5. **A quiet corner:** a recoverable focus timer tied to a chosen task.
6. **Your journey:** milestone badges, recent wins, best momentum streak, and focused minutes.
7. **Low-friction capture:** search, keyboard shortcuts, mobile bottom navigation, and clean defaults.
8. **Trustworthy recovery:** private sign-in, cloud conflict protection, exports, and preserved book history.

## Good next additions — NOT implemented
- **Weekly boss quests:** a multi-step project with a weekly finish line, not pressure to do everything daily.
- **Skill trees:** mind/body/craft/life branches that unlock cosmetic themes, never essential functionality.
- **Weekly reflection:** a short guided review of what helped, what got in the way, and what to simplify next week.
- **Rest-day tokens:** intentional pauses that preserve a streak without inventing completed activity.
- **Calendar planning and drag/drop:** time-block tasks around actual availability.
- **PWA install, offline-first startup, and optional reminders:** would need a service worker and notification permission.
- **Opt-in Notion or wearable integration:** only after choosing authoritative data, permissions, deduplication, and clear sync ownership.
- **Smarter suggestions:** propose smaller versions of difficult habits, with explicit acceptance—never silently change a routine.

Avoid public leaderboards, shame-based streak loss, punitive health mechanics, and pay-to-win rewards. The app should support a life, not become another job.
